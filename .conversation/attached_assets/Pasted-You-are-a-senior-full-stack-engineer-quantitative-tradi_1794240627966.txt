16. FINANCIAL CALCULATION ENGINE
==================================================

This is a critical system component.

Create a dedicated analytics/calculation module.

Use consistent definitions.

WIN RATE:
Winning closed trades / all non-breakeven closed trades.

Also show the number of breakeven trades and define the methodology clearly.

EXPECTANCY:
Average realized R across all eligible closed trades.

Alternatively:
Win probability × average winning R
minus
Loss probability × absolute average losing R

Include breakeven outcomes consistently.

PROFIT FACTOR:
Gross positive net P&L / absolute gross negative net P&L.

Handle zero-loss cases safely.

REALIZED R:
Net realized trade P&L / initial monetary risk.

Capture initial risk at trade entry and preserve it.

Do not retroactively redefine initial risk after stop-loss modifications.

If initial risk is unavailable or zero, realized R must be marked unavailable, not invented.

MAXIMUM DRAWDOWN:
Largest peak-to-trough decline in the selected equity series.

Use a chronological series with an explicitly defined starting balance.

R MULTIPLES:
Use a standard sign convention:
- Profitable trades are positive
- Losing trades are negative

Handle commissions, funding, swaps, and slippage consistently.

ASSET-SPECIFIC CALCULATIONS:
Do not assume Forex, XAUUSD, futures, spot crypto, and inverse contracts share the same P&L formula.

Model contract specifications and quote currencies explicitly.

Require sufficient instrument metadata to calculate trustworthy P&L.

For manual entry, allow verified broker-reported realized P&L when exact contract specifications are unavailable.

Clearly show whether P&L was calculated or user-supplied.

Create automated tests for financial calculations.
